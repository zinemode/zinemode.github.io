ZineMode

Read any article like a magazine.

ZineMode turns the web page you’re reading into paged, multi-column spreads, with a proper headline, drop cap and justified text. Built for Safari on iPad and Mac. No app required.

Recommended for Safari

Userscript

Adds a small ZineMode button to article pages. Updates itself, and works on sites that block bookmarklets.

  1. Install the free Userscripts app on your iPad or Mac, turn on its Safari extension and allow it on all websites.
  2. Open the link below in Safari, then tap the Userscripts icon and install ZineMode. Or, in Userscripts, choose New Remote and paste this address: {{USERSCRIPT_URL}}
ZineMode.user.js

Keeping scripts in iCloud Drive? Userscripts can only read files that are downloaded, so long-press the folder in Files and choose Keep Downloaded.

Nothing to install

Bookmarklet

A bookmark that turns on ZineMode. Works in any browser, and syncs to your iPad through iCloud.

ZineMode

Mac: show the Favorites bar (⇧⌘B) and drag the red button onto it.

iPad and iPhone
  1. Tap Copy code.
  2. Bookmark this page: Share → Add Bookmark, save it in Favorites.
  3. Open your bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark, rename it ZineMode, and replace its address with the copied code.
  4. For one-tap access, turn on Settings → Apps → Safari → Show Favorites Bar.

If ZineMode is already in your Mac’s Favorites with iCloud bookmarks on, it’s already on your iPad.

Turn pages

Swipe, tap the left or right third of the screen, or use the arrow keys and space bar.

Adjust

The faint ☰ at the top right has text size and light, sepia and dark themes.

Leave

Exit full screen (Safari’s ✕ or Esc) and the original page is back, right where you were.

Pick up where you left off

ZineMode remembers your page. Open it on the same article again to resume.

Keyboard

With the userscript, ⌥⇧Z opens ZineMode on any page. F toggles full screen.

Private

Everything happens in your browser. No accounts, no tracking. The only request ZineMode makes is the daily version check.
